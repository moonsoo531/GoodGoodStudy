## App Review notes draft

No app login or subscription is required. The production app contains no textbook or question-bank content. Separate original review packages will be supplied through a publicly reachable review URL or App Review attachment; never point reviewers to private user resources.

1. Launch and use the three home entries, or switch modules in the menu. Settings offers Chinese, Japanese and English, Import Help, About and optional iCloud sync.
2. Download the separate original study and quiz ZIPs into Files. Use Import ZIP inside the corresponding module. Do not unzip them before selecting them.
3. In Study, open the sample lesson, show/hide readings, toggle keyword/full-text masking, play the included synthetic audio, and save a note.
4. In Quiz, import the separate sample quiz ZIP, start a short practice and view the result. No external credentials or paid content are needed.
5. Local playback can import the supplied sample audio and subtitle files. On iOS this is an audio player.
6. iCloud is optional and off by default. It requires iCloud Drive, the same Apple Account on the devices and correctly signed builds. The app remains usable without it. In-progress quiz answers and separate player files do not sync.
